Encore – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy covers the Encore karaoke app (Encore: Karaoke & Sing Along), published on Google Play by Osli software.

We, the developer, do not collect, store on a server, or sell any personal information. Your songs, lyrics and recordings are processed on your phone and never uploaded. The free version shows ads from Google, and Google collects some data to show them; this is explained below.

What stays on your device

  • Songs you add: the app copies the audio file you pick into its own private storage, removes the singer's voice on your phone, and keeps the resulting music and voice tracks there.
  • Lyrics you paste or load, and the timings you tap.
  • Your recordings: your voice is recorded with the microphone only while you press Record on the Sing screen, and the recording is saved in the app's private storage.
  • Your settings and whether you own Encore Plus.

None of this is sent anywhere by the app. A recording only leaves your phone if you choose to save or share it yourself. Phone backups do not include the app's data.

Ads

The free version shows a banner ad at the top of some screens, served by Google AdMob. There are no ads while you sing. Encore Plus removes all ads, and the ads code is then not started at all.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google receives information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type and app version. Ads may be personalised only if you allow it. Google, not the developer, handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The developer never receives your advertising ID or any data that identifies you.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice first (Google's consent message), and you can choose Do not consent. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Encore Plus is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; the app never sees your card or payment details.

Permissions (Android)

  • Microphone: to record your voice while you sing. Only used on the Sing screen, after you press Record.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show progress while the voice is removed from a song, so the work can finish when you leave the app.
  • Internet: for ads, the consent message and Google Play purchases. Your songs and recordings are never sent over the internet.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google's ads code, as described above.

The app reads only the audio or lyrics files you pick yourself in your phone's file picker. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or photos.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account or sign-in, and never asks for your name or email address.
  • It contains no analytics or tracking code of its own.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not download, sell or suggest songs or lyrics. The few demo songs included are Creative Commons music, credited in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes every song, lyric and recording it saved. Recordings you saved or shared outside the app stay wherever you put them.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the change reaches the app.

Contact
